What we're asking for

Families use this road every day. Speeding and litter need attention.

1Speed ramps to slow traffic, especially near the estate entrance.
2A curved mirror where you can't see what's coming when you pull out.
3Bins for litter and dog waste near the coffee shop and graveyard.

Ask the council to assess the road and give us a timeframe.
