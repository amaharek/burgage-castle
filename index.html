How it works

  • Nothing is sent from this site. Each button opens the email, already written, in your own app — Apple Mail, Gmail, Outlook, whichever you use. You read it and press Send yourself.
  • Type your details once. Your name, address and phone carry over between the pages and stay on your own device.
  • Send from your own address. The council logs and answers each email separately, and identical anonymous emails get ignored — so the few edits you make in your own words are the part that counts.
  • More than one is fine. They go to different sections of the council, so one doesn't crowd out the other.

Something else need adding?
