Burgage More site · Blessington

No more late-night pours

Four ready-made emails: three to Wicklow County Council and one to our councillors and TDs. Add your name and address, tap each button, check the email and press Send. It takes a few minutes.

What the contractor asked us

Next week they need to pour 10 apartment floors, and asked residents to pick one of these:

22:00–23:00Option 1: two pours, each finishing late at night
17:00–19:00Option 2: five pours, each running into the evening

Our answer: neither. The work should stay within the site's approved hours.

Step 1

Your details

Add your name. The council won't act on unsigned complaints.
Add your address so the council knows you're directly affected.
Known late finishes are filled in. Add any others you noticed, one per line.
How is it affecting your household? (tick any)

Nothing is sent from this page. Your details stay on your phone and only go out when you press Send in your own email app.

Step 2

Send all four

Each goes to a different place, so each one gets logged and answered separately. The council emails also copy in the Director of Services for Housing, whose section is the contractor's client.

Step 3

Keep it going

  • Keep a log of each late finish: the date, the time work actually stopped, a short video with the time showing, and how it affected your household (sleep, children, health).
  • Send again each time it happens, adding the new date. Repeat complaints carry weight.
  • Keep the council's replies. If its final response to the formal complaint doesn't fix things, you can take it to the Ombudsman.
  • Prefer to phone? The council's Housing Directorate is on 0404 20120.

Share with neighbours

The more households that send these, the harder they are to ignore.
